See the cut before the machine makes it

A G-code viewer for FluidNC and GRBL machines: the toolpaths in 3D, the stock carved as the job plays, parametric G-code expanded, and the running job followed on the machine. Free, and it runs entirely in your browser.

What it shows you

Built around FluidNC, and fine with any GRBL-style G-code.

The machined part

The stock is carved with each tool's real shape as the job plays: flat, ball, bull nose, V-bit, chamfer and tapered ball.

Real-time playback

Each move takes its estimated time from feeds, rapid rates and acceleration. Scrub, step, or play at up to 500x.

Parametric G-code

FluidNC variables, expressions, conditionals and loops are expanded, and the plain G-code can be downloaded.

Messages, M0 and probing

(MSG, …) and (PRINT, …) messages appear as a pendant would show them, M0 stops wait for Resume, and Z probes touch the stock.

Problems flagged

Rapid moves that cut into the stock, and cuts deeper than a tool's flutes, are listed with their lines.

Jobs in several files

A job split by tool builds on the earlier files (PriorJob), so each one starts from the stock the last one left. Try the whole demo job in one file, or its V-bit file on the earlier bits' cuts.
